# Chronicle of HALP

## Setting: Shadows over the Sword Coast: The Netherese Cipher (AI-Determined Dynamic Atmosphere)
- World: standard forgotten realms classic dnd 5e
- Narrative Tone Directive: Authentic D&D 5e High Fantasy Adventure. Ancient Netherese ruins, perilous wilderness trails, and high-stakes faction intrigue with visceral danger and tactical realism.
- Starting Location: The Crag of Whispers - Netherese Excavation Site
- Location Atmosphere: Rain-slicked grey granite ruins jut from the pine-shrouded ridge of the Sword Mountains. Fallen columns carved with floating Netherese glyphs lie half-buried in brambles, while a collapsed stone stairway descends into a subterranean burial vault.
- Racial Traits: Versatile Adaptation: Extra skill proficiency and versatile training., Resourceful: Driven and ambitious, adaptable to any climate or discipline.
- Character Backstory: HALP was suddenly teleported to a fantasy realm. He helped that world's "main character" win, only to be instantly thrown into a new reality with his stats and gear wiped to zero.

He realized his curse: he is a wanderer forced to aid heroes across the multiverse. After every completed quest—even when he once tried helping a villain instead—he is ripped away. Though his items reset, his mind retains it all. Across countless lives, HALP has mastered legendary martial arts and fused universal arcane secrets into his own deadly, chaotic magic style.

Recently, the cycle broke. For the first time ever, HALP and his hero were defeated. Expecting death, he was instead pulled into this world. Armed with lifetimes of experience, HALP senses a profound shift. After an eternity as the sidekick, he believes this realm is where his endless loop will finally end.
